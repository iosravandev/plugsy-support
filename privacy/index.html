Plugsy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Plugsy is made by Samir Orujov. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Plugsy on your iPhone.

The short version: Plugsy has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don't collect your personal information. What you create in Plugsy stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your choices: which character plays when you plug in or unplug, your setup progress, when Plugsy's Shortcuts action last ran, and your haptics setting. Plugsy stores these on your device so the app and your Shortcuts automations can use them.
  • Your recordings: if you record your own sound, the audio file is saved in Plugsy's storage on your iPhone. Plugsy doesn't upload it anywhere. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), depending on your backup settings. You can delete a recording at any time in Plugsy. Deleting the app also deletes its recordings.

Microphone

Plugsy asks for microphone access only when you tap Record, and uses the microphone only while you're recording. You can turn access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Charger state

While Plugsy's setup screen is open, Plugsy reads whether your iPhone is charging to check that your automation works. This stays on your device.

Purchases

Optional character packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. Plugsy receives only Apple's confirmation of which packs your Apple Account owns, and never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Shortcuts

If you add Plugsy's actions to a Shortcuts automation, iOS runs them on your device. Plugsy can't see or change your automations.

Voices

Characters speak using voices built into your iPhone. The text is spoken on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't have servers that receive your data.

Children

Plugsy doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date.
