Setting up the charger automation

Plugsy plays its reactions through a Charger automation that you create in Apple's Shortcuts app. In Plugsy, open Set up automation on Home and follow the steps for your iOS version.

  • iOS 17, 18 and 26: Shortcuts → Automation → + → Charger → choose Is Connected (or Is Disconnected) → Run Immediately → add Play Plugsy Reaction and set its Event.
  • iOS 27 and later: create a shortcut with Play Plugsy Reaction, then Edit → Automation → Charger. Turn on Allow Running When Locked in Edit → Info → Privacy.

Keep Plugsy open and plug in or unplug: Plugsy checks the automation and marks it Working when it runs.

Nothing plays

  • Turn up the media volume and check whether Bluetooth headphones are connected.
  • In Shortcuts, check that the automation is turned on, runs without asking, and uses only the right Charger option.
  • A shortcut in the Shortcuts tab only runs by hand. It needs a Charger automation to run when you charge.
  • Just restarted your iPhone? Unlock it once; your own recordings stay locked until then.

Purchases

Character packs are one-time purchases tied to your Apple Account. To get them back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open Plugsy's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.
